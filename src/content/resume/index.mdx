## Summary

## Experience

### Software Engineer (official title: Data Engineer) · Warren County Public Schools · Bowling Green, KY
*Sep 2021 – Present*

- Built identity provisioning for **21,000 active users** across **40+ applications** on Rapid Identity (JavaScript/Rhino, Java, SQL), including about 14 direct integrations (Entra ID, Active Directory, Google Workspace, Clever via SFTP, and others).
- Replaced a nightly full load with an all-day **delta sync** (live July 2023) over Munis (HR) and Infinite Campus (SIS). New-hire accounts went from a **worst case of 48 hours to about 1 minute**, and daily sync data volume fell by **about 82%**.
- Built a **.NET/EF + Next.js control plane** that disables, merges and resets accounts, and flags orphaned, duplicate and mismatched records. After it launched, account-access ticket resolution fell from **1.5 to 0.6 days** (ticket export, May 2024 – May 2026).
- My provisioning code, configuration and design were **deployed statewide by the Kentucky Department of Education and Identity Automation** (171 districts, ~750,000 users) with one change, the OneRoster API for student data. I advised on the rollout.
- Designed **group-driven access**: request forms add and remove group members, granting and revoking app permissions. This automated contractor onboarding and elevated access, and let HR resend account-claim links without a helpdesk ticket. Added nightly **GitHub Actions checks** on provisioning SQL (row count, run time, schema).

- Matched deposits with a sliding window over store, date and time of day, handling multiple daily closeouts and next-day deposits. Acting on pending rather than posted transactions caught discrepancies **about a day** sooner.
