We teach staff to be suspicious of emails that ask them to click a link to activate an account. Then our own system sent them exactly that email, plain and unbranded, and they were suspicious. They were right to be.

The notification emails our provisioning system sent looked like spam. Each was put together differently, and none of them looked like they came from the district.

## The fix

I built a small templating system on Astro so every notification came from the same set of templates: consistent branding, a clear sender, and the same layout every time. Astro let me write the templates as components and render them to plain HTML for sending.

## The result

The emails went from looking like phishing to being trusted. People opened them and followed the instructions, which was the whole point of sending them.

It's an unglamorous fix. But an account nobody claims is not much better than no account, and the email is where most people meet our system for the first time.
